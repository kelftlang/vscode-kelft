# Kelft name and artwork

The Kelft name, approved brand mark, and official artwork identify the Kelft
project. Software and documentation licenses do not grant trademark rights.
This policy does not assert that a trademark has been registered.

You may accurately describe compatibility, identify the origin of unmodified
Kelft software, and link to the project. Community tools should identify their
own maintainer and avoid implying official endorsement. Forks must clearly
identify modifications and use distinct product branding when distributing a
modified product as their own. Descriptive references to Kelft remain permitted.

Approved artwork included with official documentation or the official extension
may be redistributed unchanged with those artifacts for identification. This is
not a general artwork license. Obtain separate permission for other promotional
uses, merchandise, or altered marks. Third-party brand rights remain separate.

The branding source is `kelftlang/kelft-branding`. Official styling is lowercase
`kelft`, with the tagline “Intent, compiled.” Follow its clear-space, contrast,
and geometry guidance. Do not assume that fonts or editable wordmarks are
covered by the code license. Asset provenance accompanies each included asset.
